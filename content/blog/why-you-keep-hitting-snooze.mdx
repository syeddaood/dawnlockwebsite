If you snooze every morning despite genuinely wanting to get up, the
problem isn't motivation. It's that the decision to snooze isn't really
a decision — it's a reflex, made by a brain that's barely online.

## Your brain isn't fully awake when you hit snooze

The first few minutes after an alarm goes off, your prefrontal cortex — the
part responsible for planning, judgment, and future consequences — is still
largely offline. The part that *is* active is the one looking for the
fastest way to make an unpleasant sound stop. Snooze is almost always that
fastest option, which is exactly why it's so easy to hit without really
"choosing" to.

By the time you're awake enough to regret it, you've already lost ten or
twenty minutes, usually to low-quality, fragmented sleep that makes the next
wake-up harder, not easier.

## The real decision happens the night before

Here's the part most people miss: you don't decide to snooze in the moment.
You decide it's an option the night before, the moment you leave the alarm
editable and dismissible. By 6am, there's nothing left to decide — your
half-asleep brain just exercises the option you left open for it.

This is why "just use more willpower" doesn't work. You're asking
tomorrow-morning-you, who is barely conscious, to out-negotiate a habit.
That's not a fair fight.

## What actually breaks the cycle

- **Move the phone out of arm's reach.** Reduces the easiest form of
  snoozing — the blind, half-asleep tap — down to zero.
- **Require something that needs a working brain.** A math problem, typing
  a sentence, a short memory game — anything that can't be done on
  autopilot forces just enough cognitive engagement to break the reflex.
- **Remove the option before it exists.** The most reliable fix isn't
  resisting the urge to snooze — it's not having an easy way to act on it at
  all.

That last point is the whole premise behind Cukkoo: alarms lock themselves
hours before they ring, and the only way to stop one is to finish a real
wake-up mission. There's no snooze button to reflexively hit, because
there's no snooze button.
